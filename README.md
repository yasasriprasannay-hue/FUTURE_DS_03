# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing funnel performance using the Bank Marketing Dataset. The objective is to understand customer conversion behavior, identify high-performing marketing channels, and generate actionable business insights to improve conversion rates.

## Problem Statement

Businesses invest significant resources in marketing campaigns, but not all customer interactions lead to conversions. This project aims to analyze the marketing funnel and identify factors that influence successful customer conversions.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Bank Marketing Dataset

The dataset contains customer demographics, campaign details, contact methods, and subscription outcomes from a marketing campaign.

### Features Used

* Age
* Job
* Marital Status
* Education
* Contact Method
* Campaign Month
* Subscription Outcome (y)

## Key Findings

* The dataset contains 45,211 customer records.
* Total successful conversions were 5,289.
* Overall conversion rate was approximately 11.7%.
* Cellular contact methods generated the highest conversions.
* Customers aged 30–50 contributed significantly to conversions.
* Conversion performance varied across occupations and education levels.
* Certain months showed stronger campaign performance.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize high-performing communication channels.
2. Focus on customer segments with higher conversion rates.
3. Optimize campaign timing based on monthly trends.
4. Develop personalized marketing strategies.
5. Improve customer follow-up processes.
